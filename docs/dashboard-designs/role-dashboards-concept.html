How every role dashboard is built
One line firstThe briefing tells the person what needs them now, with one button to act.
Four numbers, no moreEach tile is clickable and opens its detail, so numbers never sit as dead ends.
One focus areaThe work this role does most: decide, fix, tune or analyse. Everything else waits.
The rest on request“More views” lists every panel the role can open, so nothing is hidden and nothing crowds the screen.
